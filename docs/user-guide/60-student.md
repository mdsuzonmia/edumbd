# 60 — Student

This guide is for **Students**. Routes are under `/student` and require the `student` role.

## Dashboard

**Dashboard** (`/student/dashboard`) — overview of your academic information and quick links.

## Profile

**Profile** (`/student/profile`) — view and update your details (contact information, etc.).

## Results

**Results** (`/student/results`) — view your published exam results per term/exam.

- Individual results and transcripts are also available under the examination student area
  (`/examination/student/result`, `/examination/student/transcript`) after results are published
  by your school.
- Transcripts can be **downloaded as PDF**.

> Results appear only once the school has **generated** and **published** them — see
> [80 — Examinations & Results](80-examinations.md).


## Common questions

**I can't see my result yet.** Results must be generated and published by the school owner first.

**Can I download my transcript?** Yes — once available, use the PDF download button in the
transcript view.
