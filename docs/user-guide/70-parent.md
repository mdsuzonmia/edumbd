# 70 — Parent

This guide is for **Parents**. Routes are under `/parent` and require the `parent` role.

## Dashboard

**Dashboard** (`/parent/dashboard`) — an overview of your child's academic activity and quick
links.

## Profile

**Profile** (`/parent/profile`) — view and update your own contact details.

## Monitoring your child

Use the following screens to keep track of your child's school life:

- **Results** (`/parent/results`) — your child's published exam results.
- **Routines** (`/parent/routines`) — your child's weekly class timetable.
- **Syllabus** (`/parent/syllabus`) — what your child is currently studying.
- **Attendance** (`/parent/attendance`) — your child's attendance record.

## Common questions

**Why don't I see result data?** Results are visible after the school generates and publishes
them (see [80 — Examinations & Results](80-examinations.md)).

**Is my data safe?** Yes — access is strictly controlled by your role; you only see the students
linked to your account.
